# PRODUCT.md: Pure SaaS Product Truth & Governance

Pure SaaS is an open-source, universal AI-agent skill and frontend engineering toolkit. It guides AI coding assistants in generating original, production-quality UI/UX designs while eliminating repetitive AI design clichés across websites, dashboards, mobile apps, desktop apps, e-commerce, and design systems.

---

## 1. Product Mission & Value Proposition

- **The Problem:** AI coding models default to repetitive templates, generic purple/cyan gradient text, excessive glassmorphism blur, identical 3-column card grids, hardcoded hex values, and desktop-first layouts that break on mobile viewports.
- **The Solution:** Pure SaaS provides AI agents with deep visual intelligence, hardware-grade craft rules, an 8-step build loop, surface mode calibration, 3-tier design token enforcement, headless accessibility primitives, and real browser verification.
- **The Core Ambition:** Frontend output that impresses seasoned designers and lead engineers: Apple-inspired refinement, Impeccable-level craft, bold creative direction, excellent typography, distinctive layouts, and purposeful motion.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Personas

1. **Founders & Indie Hackers:** Building ambitious SaaS products, developer tools, or AI startups who need elite-level design without hiring an agency.
2. **Product Engineers:** Developers building full-stack applications who want cohesive design tokens, responsive layouts, and zero visual debt.
3. **AI Coding Agents:** Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, GitHub Copilot, and Gemini / Antigravity seeking clear, unambiguous architectural constraints and verification tooling.

---

## 3. Surface Modes Architecture

Pure SaaS calibrates ambition across 4 distinct surface modes:

- **Mode 1: Persuade (Landing & Marketing):** Full creative commitment, distinctive value thesis, asymmetrical spotlight layouts, real interactive previews or 3D canvasses, and high conversion narrative.
- **Mode 2: Operate (Dashboards & Consoles):** Precision, spatial density, low visual noise, restrained color accents, sub-millisecond perceived latency, and keyboard ergonomics.
- **Mode 3: Read (Docs & Publishing):** Typographic calm, comfortable reading rhythm (65–75ch), high readability, and effortless wayfinding.
- **Mode 4: Experience (Interactive & 3D):** Tactile physics, procedural Three.js/WebGL models, and direct manipulation.

---

## 4. Engineering & Safety Contracts

- **100% Local Execution:** Pure SaaS runs entirely on the user's machine. Zero external API calls, zero telemetry, and zero client-side production bundle bloat.
- **Backend Preservation:** Refinement and redesign passes strictly preserve existing backend API routes, authentication flows, database schemas, and automated test IDs.
- **Continuous Honest Verification:** Never claim tests or screenshot comparisons that were not performed. Distinguish verified results from incomplete or untested work.
